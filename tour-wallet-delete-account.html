Tour Wallet — Delete your account & data

App: Tour Wallet (Md Apple Mahmud) · Last updated: 02 June 2026

You can permanently delete your Tour Wallet account and all associated data by sending an email request. We process deletions within 7 business days.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to iapplee007@gmail.com from the same email address linked to your Tour Wallet account.
  2. Subject line: Tour Wallet — Delete my account
  3. In the body, include: “Please delete my Tour Wallet account and all associated data.”
  4. We will reply within 7 business days confirming the deletion.

What is NOT deleted

Trip documents created by other admins, and contributions / expenses / chat messages you added inside those trips, are not removed — doing so would corrupt the shared expense history for the other trip members. However, your identifiers in those records are stripped: your name is replaced with “[removed user]” and your user ID is cleared. The other trip members can no longer link any data back to you.

If you want a specific message, expense, or contribution removed entirely from a trip you no longer participate in, mention the trip name and item in your deletion email and we will review on a case-by-case basis.

Alternatives to full deletion

  • Leave a trip — you can leave any individual trip from the trip menu. Your account stays active for your other trips.
  • Turn off push notifications — in Profile → Push notifications, flip the switch to Off. Your push token is removed from our database.
