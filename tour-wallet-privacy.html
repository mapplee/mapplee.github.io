Tour Wallet — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 02 June 2026 · Contact: iapplee007@gmail.com

Tour Wallet (“the app”, “we”) is a mobile app that helps small groups of friends track shared trip expenses in Bangladeshi Taka (BDT). This page explains what data the app collects, how it is used, where it is stored, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what the app needs to function. Specifically:

DataWhy we collect itSource
Email addressTo create your account and let you sign in across devices.You provide it (email/password sign-up or Google Sign-In).
Display nameSo other members of your trip can recognise who paid or contributed.You provide it.
Push notification tokenTo deliver in-app notifications (e.g. an expense was added). Optional — you can turn it off in Profile.Issued by Expo / Firebase Cloud Messaging when you allow notifications.
Trip data (members, contributions, expenses, settlements, chat messages)Core feature of the app — tracking who paid what on a shared trip.You and the other members of your trip enter it.
Personal expenses (private)So you can keep track of personal spending alongside group expenses.You enter it. Visible only to you.

We do not collect: precise location, contacts, photos, microphone audio, advertising identifiers, browsing history, or any data about apps installed on your device.

2. How we use the data

  • To run the app: store and sync your trips, contributions, and expenses across the devices of trip members.
  • To send push notifications about activity in your trips, if you have notifications turned on.
  • To compute who owes whom and suggest minimum-transfer settlements.
  • To detect crashes and fix bugs (aggregated, no personal content).

We do not sell or rent your data. We do not show advertisements. We do not use your data to train any AI model.

3. Where data is stored

Data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore (a Google Cloud product) as part of the Firebase project tour-app-2026. Authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication. Push notifications are relayed through Expo Push Service and Firebase Cloud Messaging. These services are operated by Google LLC and Expo / Software Mansion respectively, under their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Cloud — cloud.google.com/terms/cloud-privacy-notice
  • Expo — expo.dev/privacy

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS / TLS) and at rest by the cloud provider.

4. Who can see your data

  • Trip data: visible only to the members of that specific trip. Firestore security rules enforce this on the server side.
  • Personal expenses: visible only to you, even if tagged against a trip.
  • Chat messages: visible only to the members of the trip the message belongs to.
  • We (the developer) do not browse your trip content. We may access aggregated logs only to debug a specific issue you have reported.

5. Data retention and deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by writing to iapplee007@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account. Deletion includes your user profile, push tokens, personal expenses, and your membership in trips. Trip documents owned by other admins are not deleted, but your identifiers are removed from them.

You may also leave individual trips from the trip menu; doing so removes you from that trip but does not delete your account.

6. Children

Tour Wallet is intended for general audiences and is suitable for ages 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us and we will delete the account.

7. Security

All network traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. Authentication tokens are stored in encrypted device storage. Server-side access is restricted by Firestore security rules: a user can only read or write documents they are explicitly authorised for.

8. Permissions the app requests

  • Internet — to sync your trip data and send notifications.
  • Network state — to show an offline banner when you have no connection.
  • Camera — only when you tap “Scan QR” to join a trip. Camera frames are processed on-device and never uploaded.
  • Notifications — only if you grant the OS prompt. Used to tell you when a trip member added an expense, requested approval, etc.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy materially we will update the “Last updated” date at the top and, where reasonable, surface a notice in the app.
